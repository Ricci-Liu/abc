Grammar operations in this room

Tense
Past and future collapse into the present.
was / will be → is

Verb Tenses
Ongoing and completed actions reduce to base form.
waiting / waited → wait
going / went → go

Plurality
Plural markers are removed.
keys → key

Articles (A/An/The)
Indefinite and definite collapse into one.
a / an / the → the

All messages are processed by a fixed sequence of grammatical rules.
Gaps in grammar and limits of the system may produce unexpected transformations—some affect comprehension, others do not.
